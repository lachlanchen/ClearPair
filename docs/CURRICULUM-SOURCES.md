# Curriculum references

Lessons and diagrams are originally authored. References inform factual checks;
their media, figures and transcripts are not bundled in the apps.

- [Hadar Shemesh: American English vowel chart lesson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9E6F57s-V7U)
  is the owner's requested reference. Captions were retrieved for private study.
  Video media download was attempted with yt-dlp and its supported JavaScript
  challenge component, but the provider returned HTTP 403 twice. **The video file
  was not successfully downloaded or watched locally.** The app uses its own vowel
  diagrams and lessons; no creator footage has been redistributed.
- [Creator's related vowel-resource page](https://hadarshemesh.com/podcast/138/)
  links to the requested lesson. The design distinguishes vowel quality, position
  and movement; length alone is not used to teach or grade English vowel contrasts.
- [University of Iowa Sounds of Speech](https://soundsofspeech.uiowa.edu/) and
  [IPA phonetics resources](https://www.internationalphoneticassociation.org/content/links-phonetics-resources)
  are references for articulatory terminology, not artwork sources.
- [UCLA Mandarin phonetics](https://phonetics.ucla.edu/appendix/languages/chinese/chinese.html)
  informs the distinction between Mandarin sibilant series and syllabic qualities.
- [National Institute of Korean Language: consonant principles](https://www.korean.go.kr/eng_hangeul/principle/002.html)
  documents the plain, aspirated and tense consonant families.
  [Official pronunciation rules](https://www.korean.go.kr/front/page/pageView.do?page_id=P000098)
  are relevant to context and syllable-final behaviour.
- [Madinah Arabic: connected letter forms](https://madinaharabic.com/free-content/reading/lesson-1/part-16)
  informs Arabic joining instruction. This course uses common modern standard print
  and distinguishes dots from short-vowel marks. Letter names are not the same as
  the isolated consonant sounds.

## Important caveats

- Some requested examples are not minimal pairs; label them as word/syllable
  comparisons rather than claiming exactly one changed phoneme.
- Cot/caught and Korean ㅐ/ㅔ can merge. Do not grade a valid merger as an error.
- Korean ㄱ/ㅋ/ㄲ and related series need three-way sound evidence and context,
  not a one-dimensional “more air” rule.
- Arabic visual letter identification, letter-name pronunciation and consonant
  production are three separate tasks; scores must say which was assessed.
- Pinyin zi/zhi/ji do not share one identical vowel. Mandarin h differs from English h.
- Mandarin tone references must allow contextual sandhi and a low third tone.
- The guides are schematic. There is no validated 3D anatomy model in this preview.
- Bundled synthetic voices need phonetic auditioning, especially isolated polyphonic
  Hanzi, English homographs, unfamiliar syllables and Arabic diacritics.
